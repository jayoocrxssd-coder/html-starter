Moment Studio is a luxury event decoration service available for hotel rooms, private homes, and outdoor spaces across Central Florida. We believe that extraordinary celebrations don't require extraordinary effort on your part — just a vision and the right team.

Born from a love of beautiful moments

Moment Studio was built on a simple belief: that the spaces we celebrate in should feel as special as the occasions themselves. We started in Orlando with a handful of setups and a deep passion for detail.

Today, we serve couples, families, and organizations across Central Florida — bringing cinematic, personalized decoration to every event, from intimate proposals to full venue transformations.

Every arrangement we create is designed from scratch, tailored to your story, your aesthetic, and your person.
