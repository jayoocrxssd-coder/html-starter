The founder
Where the love
for detail began

Moment Studio was born from a deep love of creating spaces that feel intentional, warm, and completely personal. After years of watching events fall flat — not because of the people, but because the details weren't there — I decided to build something better.

What started as a passion for styling beautiful tablescapes and intimate setups grew into a full-service studio. Today, Moment Studio handles everything from the first consultation to the final breakdown, so you can be fully present for the moments that matter most.

Every event we touch is treated as if it were our own. We don't do cookie-cutter. We don't do generic. We do yours.
