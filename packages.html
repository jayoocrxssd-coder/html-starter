Common questions
Before you inquire
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks before your event, especially for weekends. Popular dates fill quickly — the earlier you reach out, the better we can serve you.
How does pricing work?
All packages are custom-quoted based on your event details, size, and vision. There's no one-size-fits-all price list — we want to give you an accurate number, not a guess.
Do you travel outside of Orlando?
Yes. We serve the greater Central Florida area and are available for travel beyond. Travel fees may apply — just mention your location in your inquiry and we'll let you know.
What's included in setup and breakdown?
We handle all delivery, placement, and installation before your event — and we return to fully break everything down after. You don't lift a finger.
Can I mix elements from different packages?
Absolutely. During your consultation we'll discuss your vision in full and build a custom scope from there. No package is set in stone — it's all tailored to you.
What if I don't know exactly what I want?
That's what we're here for. Share your inspiration, your colors, your feeling — and we'll translate it into a cohesive décor plan. You don't need to have all the answers yet.
