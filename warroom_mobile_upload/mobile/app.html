Docs · FAQ · contact CRES Ventures
Top questions
How does AXIS know about my business?⌄
AXIS reads only what's inside your War Room — missions, P&L entries, notes, calendar. It never reads outside data unless you explicitly connect a service. All processing runs against your data, scoped to your account.
Is my data private?⌄
Yes. War Room is local-first with encrypted cloud sync. Your data is yours; we don't sell it, train on it, or share it. End-to-end encryption is on by default for everything in your vault.
Can I run multiple businesses?⌄
Yes — unlimited businesses on every plan. Each business gets isolated P&L, missions, calendar, and bills. Switch instantly from the topbar or with ⌘B.
How do I add a teammate?⌄
Team seats are on Small Business and up. From Settings → Workspace → Invite Members, send an invite link. Each seat gets their own private layer and shared access to mission boards you choose.
Can I cancel anytime?⌄
Anytime. Your data stays accessible (read-only) for 90 days after cancellation, then can be fully exported as JSON/CSV.
Does War Room work offline?⌄
Yes. War Room is local-first — every module works offline. Changes sync when you're back online; no data is lost.
